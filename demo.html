ANY QUESTIONS?

Here are the answers

What is Pictue and what is it used for?

Pictue is a Finnish photo documentation application built specifically for the construction, property, and energy sectors. It enables site workers to take photos in the field in just three clicks, which are automatically organized in a single location, tagged with GPS coordinates and timestamps, for use by the entire organization.

How quickly will Pictue be rolled out within our company?

Implementation can typically be completed in a few days without the need for a separate IT project. Field installers learn the app in under five minutes.

Can Pictue be integrated with our company’s existing systems?

Yes. Pictue supports turnkey integrations with Infrakit, Trimble, M-Files, Microsoft SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams, Fieldsight, Optopro, GroundHawk, Paikannin, and Microsoft Entra ID (Single Sign-On).

Does Pictue work without an internet connection on remote sites?

Yes, 100% of Pictue's core photo documentation features work offline in poor coverage, basements, or tunnels. Photos are stored securely on the device and transferred automatically once connection is restored.
