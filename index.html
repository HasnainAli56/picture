FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about Pictue

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What is Pictue and what is it used for?

Pictue is a field photo documentation application designed specifically for construction, infrastructure, and property maintenance sectors. It captures geotagged, timestamped site photos in three simple clicks and automatically organizes them on a visual map for instant access across your whole organization.

How quickly can Pictue be rolled out across our company?

Implementation typically takes just a few days. Because the mobile app requires zero complex configuration and is designed for ease of use in cold weather and active sites, field crews learn to use it in under five minutes without requiring IT-heavy deployment.

Can Pictue be integrated with our existing systems?

Yes. Pictue provides turnkey integrations with Infrakit, Trimble, M-Files, Microsoft SharePoint, OneDrive, Microsoft Teams, Fieldsight, Optopro, GroundHawk, Paikannin, and Microsoft Entra ID (Single Sign-On). We also provide REST APIs and build custom connectors for enterprise ERP systems.

Does Pictue work without an internet connection on remote sites?

Absolutely. 100% of Pictue's core documentation capabilities work offline. Technicians can capture photos, record voice notes, and log project milestones underground, in tunnels, or in remote rural areas. The moment the device reconnects to Wi-Fi or cellular service, all captured data automatically syncs to the cloud.
