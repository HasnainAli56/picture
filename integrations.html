Integrations in a nutshell

Data flows bidirectionally between Pictue and your project management, ERP, and GIS environments.

01. Connect
Link existing tools

Receive projects, user roles, utility cabinets, vehicles, or work orders directly from your ERP or GIS.

02. Capture

SUPPORTED ECOSYSTEM

Turnkey integrations with industry-leading platforms

Connect with one click or explore our developer API documentation.

Infrakit

Infrakit

Photos taken in Pictue during infrastructure field work automatically route into Infrakit folders within matching projects. Automate standardized photo naming and project tracking.

Trimble

Trimble NIS & UTG

Photos captured with Pictue are linked directly to network assets and project views in Trimble NIS and UTG, improving information availability and cross-team collaboration.

M-Files

M-Files

Ensure all maintenance photos and facility inspection records captured in Pictue flow directly into M-Files metadata-driven document management without manual intermediate steps.

SharePoint

Microsoft SharePoint

Connect your organization to SharePoint. Photos captured in the field transfer automatically and in real time into structured folder hierarchies on your enterprise SharePoint.

OneDrive

Microsoft OneDrive

Photos automatically sync into organized project directories, making high-resolution site evidence instantly available to stakeholders and managers.

Teams

Microsoft Teams

Broadcast site milestones and quality observations into dedicated Teams project channels in real time, keeping office staff synchronized with site progress.

Fieldsight

Fieldsight

Fieldsight's high-accuracy centimeter RTK GNSS photos become instantly visible and searchable within Pictue, consolidating all survey data into one system.

Optopro

Optopro Solutions AB

Captured photos transfer automatically and in real time to the correct telecom assets and fiber rollout milestones inside Optopro, providing complete status transparency.

GroundHawk

GroundHawk

Connect your GroundHawk account to view high-accuracy GPS photos and underground utility work on the same interactive map at a glance.

Paikannin

Paikannin.com

Import vehicle fleets and telematics listings directly into Pictue to match machinery positions with field photo captures automatically.

Entra ID

Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD)

Enterprise Single Sign-On (SSO). Automate user provisioning, access permissions, and secure corporate login with your organization's Microsoft credentials.
