ALL-IN-ONE PLATFORM

All you need for field photo documentation.

Explore the purpose-built features that make visual site documentation with Pictue effortless, lightning-fast, and completely dependable.

01 — THREE-CLICK SIMPLICITY

Three clicks are enough to document any site situation.

Pictue makes construction site documentation intuitive. Point, click, and your photo is instantly stored and secured for future reference.

Every photo is automatically embedded with high-precision GPS location coordinates, timestamp, photographer ID, work phase, and your organization's custom definitions without manual typing.

See three-click workflow in action →
02 — VISUAL MAP STORAGE

Simple map-based storage eliminates folders forever.

Pictue completely eliminates the need for naming files and maintaining messy folders. Photos automatically pin directly onto the interactive project map.

This makes images effortlessly findable whether you need them ten minutes later or three years into a liability dispute. Filter instantly by photographer, team role, comments, map boundary, or custom tags.

03 — STRUCTURED RETRIEVAL

Easy archiving with customized keyword paths.

In addition to automatic metadata, your organization can set up easy-to-use keyword hierarchies (e.g. Foundation → Reinforcement → Inspection).

Field installers select categories in a single tap, ensuring standardization across subcontractors and making quality audits instantaneous.

04 — REAL-TIME COLLABORATION

Seamless photo sharing between teams and subcontractors.

Up-to-date site information is always at your fingertips. Whether monitoring a subcontractor's daily progress or building client trust with verifiable milestones, Pictue bridges the gap.

Enable secure cross-company sharing without granting full internal server access. Share via password-protected web links, WhatsApp, email, or SMS directly from the mobile app.

05 — FIELD DURABILITY

100% Offline capability & voice-to-text audio comments.

Never worry about poor cellular connectivity on remote sites, basements, or tunnels. Pictue captures and timestamps photos offline and syncs automatically once back in coverage.

Hands busy in cold weather or wearing heavy gloves? Simply tap the microphone icon to speak notes directly onto the photo; Pictue transcribes speech to text automatically.
